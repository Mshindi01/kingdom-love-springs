# Notes for Kingdom Love Springs

Plain-language updates on the new website, newest first.

---

## 10 November 2026: The look and feel

**Today you can see** a "styleguide" page: a showroom of every building block the website will use, before they're put together into real pages.
- **Handwriting that writes itself.** Short phrases like *from struggle to purpose*, *light & salt* and *across Kenya* now appear as if written by hand, stroke by stroke, with a flowing pen line underneath. We tried three handwriting styles and chose **Ms Madi**: it's energetic and warm but still easy to read over photos.
- **"What we do" circles.** Five simple circles (Education, Football, Mentorship, Families, Faith). When you point at one, it fills with a photo and the word turns into handwriting.
- **Your numbers, in a big green band.** 35 children sponsored, about 200 at each gathering, 500–600 reached each year, and 3 regions. The numbers count up as you scroll to them.
- **A map of Kenya** with Nairobi, Western Kenya and the Coast marked (regions only, never exact places).
- **Cards, buttons and forms** in your colours, including the "Sponsor a child" card that will float over the main photo.

**Good to know**
- Everything also works for people who use a keyboard or a screen reader, or who turn animations off on their phone.
- Some samples are marked **"Example"** (for instance a progress bar and a Bible verse). They're there only to show the design and won't go on the real pages unless you confirm them.
- The photos are still illustrated placeholders. Real photos go in once guardian consent is recorded.

## 10 November 2026: The foundations are in place

**Today you can see** (on our computer for now; an online link will follow soon):
- A simple "New website coming soon" page with your name and your mission sentence, in your own words.
- The colours we're starting with: deep forest green, fresh leaf green, sunrise gold and red-soil clay on a warm cream background. These are placeholders until we receive your logo and brand colours, and they're easy to change.
- The lettering: a clean, modern typeface for reading, and a handwritten style for small decorative touches like *"from struggle to purpose"* and *"light & salt"*.
- A sample placeholder picture. We won't use stock photos of children. Your real photos go in once guardian consent is recorded for each one.

**Behind the scenes**
- The website is built to be fast, to work well on phones, and to be usable by people who use a keyboard or a screen reader.
- Child-protection rules are built in: the site automatically checks that children are shown by first name only, that no school names or exact locations appear, and that every photo has recorded consent.

**What we need from you** (whenever you're ready)
1. Your logo and brand colours
2. Contact details: email, phone/WhatsApp, location, social media links
3. Photos (tournaments, classrooms, mentors, families), with guardian consent recorded
4. Founder/team names, photos and short bios
5. Registration details, and partner organisations (with permission to show their logos)
6. Two or three real stories (with consent), upcoming tournaments, prayer points and volunteer roles
7. Your preferred Bible verse and translation (we suggested Matthew 5:14–16)
8. What sponsorship covers (school fees and mentorship, plus uniforms or books?) and whether to show a cost
