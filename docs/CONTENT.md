## 1. Client text (verbatim)

> Kingdom Love Springs exists to give children and youth from poor and vulnerable communities across Kenya the opportunity to thrive in school, in life, and in faith.
>
> We currently sponsor 35 children (ages 7–17) with school fees and ongoing mentorship. Our team regularly visits their schools and also meets them during school holidays to track their progress, encourage them, and walk alongside their families. Through this consistent support, children who once faced the streets or an uncertain future are now completing secondary school, improving academically, and in some cases advancing to college.
>
> Alongside education, we use sports, especially football, as a powerful tool to reach hundreds of children and youth each year. In tournaments and clinics held across Nairobi, Western Kenya, and Coast regions, young people play, build friendships, and hear life transforming Christian teaching. These gatherings, attended by around 200 children and youth on average, at a time and reaching an estimated 500 to 600 annually, create space for mentorship, hope, and a personal encounter with the love of Jesus Christ.
>
> Kingdom Love springs seeks to change and impact more destinies in this nation and beyond, and the current generation through education, sports and mentorship.
>
> Our approach is intentionally wholesome: quality education from primary through secondary, active family involvement, consistent mentorship, and the Gospel shared through sports and relationships. We believe every child and youth deserves the chance to move from struggle to purpose, and to become light and salt in their generation.

(Fix only obvious typos when using it: "Kingdom Love springs" → "Kingdom Love Springs", "life transforming" → "life-transforming".)
